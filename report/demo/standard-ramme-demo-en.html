    • How to read this

    Each bar runs from 0 to 100 and shows how much of that dimension's scale range your answers cover. The midpoint line (50) is only the middle of the scale. Above or below the midpoint does not mean 'good' or 'bad' — only that your answers sit on one or the other side of the scale's middle.

    Your scores are not compared with others (no norms) and they say nothing about what you can or cannot do. They are not percentiles and not a measure of intelligence.

    Mood and state can affect how you answer a test — how you feel while taking it can move your scores up or down. Treat the result as a snapshot, not a fixed trait.

    This report is written so that you (and anyone you share it with) can understand the result without having seen the tests. All limitations are stated here in plain text.
